Support

We read every message. Write to guardian.app2@gmail.com, or use Send feedback in the app's settings.

iPhone: the curtain doesn't appear in Safari

Open Guardian's Settings and tap the button under "Safari curtain". It opens Guardian's page in Safari's settings: switch Guardian on and set All Websites to Allow. On iOS before 26.2, go to Settings, then Apps, then Safari, then Extensions, then Guardian.

iPhone: does it work in Chrome?

Not yet. Chrome on iPhone doesn't allow extensions, so the curtain works in Safari. Strict mode in Extra protection turns on Apple's adult-content filter, which applies across the phone's browsers.

Android: the curtain doesn't appear

Guardian needs its browser monitor turned on: Settings, then Accessibility, then Installed apps, then Guardian Content Monitor. If Android says it's controlled by a restricted setting, open Guardian's App info, tap the ⋮ menu and choose "Allow restricted settings", then try again.

I slipped. Did I lose my progress?

No. A slip weathers your journey for a few clean days; it never erases it. Your jewels and items stay yours.

Can I delete my data?

Everything lives on your phone. Uninstalling Guardian deletes it. There's no account and nothing on our side to delete.
